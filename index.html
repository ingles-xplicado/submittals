Review the draft in Word, add any drawings, then export those pages as one PDF. When you click Build package, the builder automatically attaches each selected product’s saved TDS and SDS in product order.

    
The PDF library lives in the sheets/ folder of the published website. GitHub Actions downloads the official files from the catalog's links when the site is updated and refreshes them monthly. No per-package sheet uploads are needed.
